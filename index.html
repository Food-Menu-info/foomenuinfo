Taco Bell menu

Taco Bell is an American fast food chain serving Mexican-inspired food such as tacos, burritos, quesadillas and nachos. Its menu is known for value items, a Cravings Value Menu, customizable orders and late-night hours at many locations.

McDonald's menu

McDonald's is the world's largest fast food burger chain. Its menu covers burgers, chicken, breakfast, Happy Meals, McCafé coffee and desserts, with combo meals and app deals available at most US locations.

Chick-fil-A menu

Chick-fil-A is an American fast food chain that specializes in chicken sandwiches, nuggets and strips. All locations are closed on Sundays, and the menu includes breakfast, salads, signature sauces and hand-spun milkshakes.

Common questions

What is Menu Info?

Menu Info (menu-info.com) is a free website that publishes full menus for popular US restaurant chains, including Taco Bell, McDonald's, Chick-fil-A, Starbucks, Chipotle and more than 40 other brands.

Where can I see the full Taco Bell menu?

The Taco Bell menu page lists tacos, burritos, specialties, combos, sides, drinks and breakfast items, along with current deals.

Where can I see the full McDonald's menu?

The McDonald's menu page covers burgers, chicken, breakfast, Happy Meals, McCafé drinks and desserts.

Is Chick-fil-A open on Sunday?

No. Chick-fil-A restaurants are closed every Sunday. You can browse the full Chick-fil-A menu any day of the week.

Are menu prices the same at every location?

No. Most chains let franchise owners set their own prices, so prices can vary by city and state. Use the menus here as a guide and check your local restaurant or app for exact prices.

Is Menu Info connected to these restaurants?

No. Menu Info is an independent site and is not affiliated with any of the restaurant brands listed. All trademarks belong to their owners.
